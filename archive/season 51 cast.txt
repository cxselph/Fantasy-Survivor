survivor 51 cast

**Survivor 51**, titled "**The Open Era**," features **21** all-new castaways competing for $1 million, with the season premiering on **September 23, 2026**. Hosted by **Jeff Probst**, this season marks a new phase for the franchise following the milestone 50th season.

The cast includes a diverse group of professionals and personalities:
*   **Devin Way**: Actor known for *Grey’s Anatomy*.
*   **Ana Sani**: Voice actress known for *Strawberry Shortcake* and *The Boys*.
*   **Brady Booker**: Former WWE professional wrestler.
*   **Mike Pinsky**: Assistant Director of Baseball Operations for the New York Yankees.
*   **Carter Krull**: Livestock farmer from Iowa.
*   **Lewis Kelly**: Farmer living in Puerto Rico (originally from Dublin, Ireland).
*   **Maggie Nestor**: Farmer and homeschool teacher from West Virginia.
*   **Aaliyah Puglia**: Chef from New Jersey.
*   **Alexis Levine**: Criminal defense attorney.
*   **Patt Cannaday**: Federal prosecutor.
*   **Sharonda Cox**: OB/GYN resident.
*   **Kristin Flickinger**: Crisis management consultant.
*   **Eric Macksoud**: Mental health counselor.
*   **Jenna Doore**: Wedding photographer.
*   **Cristian Chavez**: Head of HR.
*   **Danny “Kilby” Kilby**: Video game designer.
*   **An “Thien An” Nguyen**: Medical student.
*   **Linnea Capobianco**: Entrepreneur.
*   **Angelica “Jelly” Loblack**: Sociology professor.
*   **Rob Antonson**: Airline gate agent.
*   **Ori Jean-Charles**: Personal trainer.
